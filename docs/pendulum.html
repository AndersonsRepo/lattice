Magnetic Pendulum Fractal
A pendulum hangs over magnets. Released from rest at each pixel, it swings chaotically before settling near one magnet. Nearby starting points can lead to completely different destinations — the boundaries between basins of attraction are fractal.

The physics: magnetic attraction + gravity restoring force + friction. Small parameter changes create wildly different fractal patterns.
