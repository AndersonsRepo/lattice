Epoch

the four seasons of evolutionary pressure

Emergence

The system favors complexity and novelty. Pieces score higher when they push entropy upward and introduce structures not yet seen. This is the epoch of experimentation — the fitness landscape rewards the strange, the dense, the surprising. Many species are born here.

Order

Symmetry and structural regularity are rewarded. The system prunes wild experiments and selects for pieces with geometric balance, repeating motifs, and clean internal logic. This is the epoch of crystallization — where chaos settles into form.

Chaos

Edge activity and entropy dominate the fitness function. The system rewards turbulence — pieces with active boundaries, fractal detail, and restless energy score highest. This is the epoch of creative destruction, where stability is penalized and volatility is virtue.

Harmony

Balance across all metrics is valued above any single extreme. Pieces must be complex but not chaotic, structured but not rigid, dense but not overwhelming. This is the epoch of maturity — the system selects for organisms that are good at everything, not great at one thing.
