Chladni Figures

Ernst Chladni (1756-1827) discovered that bowing a metal plate sprinkled with sand reveals its vibrational modes. Sand collects on the nodal lines — where the plate doesn't move.

The pattern depends on the plate shape and which mode numbers (m, n) are excited. Higher modes create finer, more intricate patterns. Superposing multiple modes creates interference patterns of extraordinary complexity.

Rectangular plate: z = sin(mπx/L) × sin(nπy/W)
Circular plate: z = Jm(kmnr) × cos(mθ)
